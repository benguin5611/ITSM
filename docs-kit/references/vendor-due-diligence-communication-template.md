# Vendor due diligence communication template

> Part of the [docs-kit](../README.md#before-you-rely-on-anything-here): a Department-of-One starting point, not a certified or audit-ready control out of the box. Read that disclaimer and this artefact's own "Adapt this to your context" section before relying on it.

The message to send a vendor along with the [Vendor Due Diligence Questionnaire](vendor-due-diligence-questionnaire.md), plus a short follow-up for when it goes quiet.

## Initial request

> **Subject:** Vendor security questionnaire from [Your organisation]
>
> Hi [vendor contact name],
>
> As part of our ongoing supplier risk management, we ask vendors who [handle our data / provide a service that touches our environment] to complete a short due diligence questionnaire. This is something we run for every vendor in your risk tier. It isn't a signal of any specific concern.
>
> Attached is the questionnaire, covering your security program, data handling, access controls, incident history, and any relevant certifications. Please return it, along with any supporting evidence (certificates, policy summaries, audit reports), by [date, typically two to three weeks from receipt].
>
> If a question doesn't apply to the service you provide us, mark it "Not applicable" rather than leaving it blank, so we know it was considered rather than overlooked.
>
> If you have any questions about the request or what we're looking for, reply to this email and we'll be glad to help.
>
> Thanks,
> [Your name]
> [Your organisation]

## Following up

If nothing arrives by the deadline, a short, friendly nudge that references the original request usually lands better than resending the whole questionnaire cold:

> Hi [name], just following up on the vendor questionnaire we sent on [date]. We'd like to get this wrapped up by [new date] if possible. Let me know if anything's unclear or if you need more time.

## Adapt this to your context

- Set the response window to match how urgently the relationship needs to close: two to three weeks is a reasonable default for a routine vendor, but a time-critical onboarding may need a shorter turnaround agreed upfront with the vendor.
- For low-risk vendors, a shorter, informal version of this message (or skipping the formal questionnaire in favour of compensating evidence) may be proportionate. See the "Adapt this to your context" sections of the questionnaire and the [Software Onboarding & Vendor Due Diligence](../procedures/software-onboarding-and-vendor-due-diligence-procedure.md) procedure.
- Keep a record of when the request and any follow-ups were sent. If diligence is ever challenged later, the paper trail of what was asked and when matters as much as the answers themselves.
